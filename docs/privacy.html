SCORZ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. This covers the SCORZ website and the SCORZ Android app.

The short version

SCORZ does not ask for your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. The app has no ads and sells nothing. You can play without an account.

What stays on your device

Your progress (level, rank, items, settings, friends list and a random friend code) is saved on your own device. Deleting the app or clearing the site data removes it.

What goes to our game server

  • Playing online: the player name you picked, your random friend code, your rank and look, your controls during a match, and chat messages go to our game server so the match can run. They are not stored after you leave.
  • An optional SCORZ account: if you make one, the server keeps your username, a scrambled (hashed) password and your game progress so you can load it on another device. Please do not use your real name as a username.
  • Reports: if you report a chat message, the message and the two player names are saved so it can be checked.
  • Like any internet service, the server sees your IP address while you are connected. We do not use it to identify you.

Counting visitors (website only)

The website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It uses no cookies and does not follow you across sites. The Android app does not include it.

Children

SCORZ is suitable for players aged 12 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Typed chat is only for signed-in players and is filtered; everyone else uses fixed quick phrases.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Our hosting companies (Render for the game server, Cloudflare for visitor counts) process it only to run the game.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear the site data to remove everything on your device. To delete a SCORZ account, write to the contact email shown on our store page and tell us the username.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
