# SCORZ (formerly EGO BLOCKS): notes for Claude Code

A voxel football game (anime "ego striker" feel, blocky art) that runs in a web browser. Quick Match and Lawless
are 3v3; Ranked grows from 3v3 to 4v4 to 5v5. Rosters are in `ROSTERS` (07_data.js): 3v3 GK DEF MID, 4v4 adds ST, 5v5 is
`SETUPS_5` picked at random per team (ultra defensive: two DEFs, ultra attacking: ST + FW). You play the most attacking
role (`leadRole`), which also takes kickoffs.
The whole game is one HTML page built from the small files in `src/`. No frameworks, no bundler.

## Commands

- `npm install` once. It gets Three.js r128 and the fonts (so the build can put them inside the page) and Playwright for tests.
- `npm run build` builds `docs/index.html`. That single file is the game: open it in Chrome or Edge to play.
- `npm test` runs a quick health check (`test/check.js`). Needs `npx playwright install chromium` once.
- `npm run test:touch` checks the phone controls with real multi-touch on an emulated phone.
- `npm run test:all` runs the main suites.
- Always run `npm run build` after editing anything in `src/`, then `npm test`.

## How the code fits together

`build.js` joins `src/00_head.html` (title, all CSS, the two root elements) and every `src/NN_*.js`
file in file-name order into ONE function scope. There are no imports or exports: a top-level
`const` in `01_core.js` is visible in every later file. Keep top-level names unique across files.

## Restarts

- `onOut` sets a THROW-IN, CORNER or GOAL KICK; `doRestart` places the taker and sets `p.setPiece` (`throw`, `corner`, `goalkick`) and `m.setPieceBy`. The taker stands still (`maxSpeed` 0), opponents keep back (`updateSetPiece`), pass/shoot play it (`takeSetPiece`, `throwBall`), bots wait `sp.wait`, a human gets 6s. A ball held for a set piece never counts as out. Test: `node test/setpieces.js`.

## Flows, Awakenings, kits, daily

- Each Flow in `FLOWS` has a `color`, an `fx` look (drawn by `FX.flow`, also on the start screen) and `mods`; `start` effects run when it switches on (`stamina`, `resetCd`).
- Each style in `STYLES` has `awaken` (name, desc, `mods`, optional `perk`: `dodge` or `tank`, and `move`: its signature move). Awakening comes by itself: `updateAwakening` rolls `awakenChance(p)` once a second, and big plays (`flowAdd` amounts of 10+) fill the hidden `p.awake` meter that raises the chance. It lasts `AWAKEN_TIME`: cooldowns reset and run x2, ability `fx` set while awakened last 50% longer, and Z (or the Flow button) does the `move` once (`awakenMove`). Online sends `aw`/`awt`/`amu` in `me` and flag 128.
- Emotes: `EMOTES` (07_data.js, shared with the server). B, the smiley touch button or D-pad down opens `#emote-pop` (`UI.toggleEmotes`, `UI.emoteKeys`): keys 1 to 9 pick; a controller moves a highlight with the D-pad (`UI.markEmote`, 3 across) and A plays it, so every emote is reachable. `Match.emote` plays the `emote` state. New emotes need an animation branch in `Player.animate` (celebrate/emote) and How to Play text. Online sends `em`. Test: `node test/emotes.js`.
- Rewards (owner's rule, Nov 2026): offline, only modes with `rewards: true` in `MODES` (Quick Match, Ranked, Lawless: full-time matches) give emeralds, XP, pass, daily and GOALS progress. King of the Hill, Volleys and Duels offline are just for fun (`fun` in `Game.finishMatch`, the results say why; they live in the MORE MODES panel, `UI.openModes`, which says so and tags the featured one). Every online friends match gives rewards. Leaving early never does. Test: `node test/featured.js`.
- Online rank gap: two or more tiers above every real player on the other team means no rewards for a win or draw (`nm.result.noReward`). Test: `node test/rankgap.js`.
- The `kit` skin slot: skins with `pattern` and `trim`, drawn on the team's colours (`makePlayerSkin`), so sides stay readable. Keepers keep their kit.
- 21 styles. Newest: SPARK (Rare, ZAP: stuns the nearest opponent within 4 blocks for 0.5s) and TITAN (Legendary, RAM: a `dashP` with `ram: true`, `Match.ramHits` knocks over opponents it touches). They come from `GOALS` steps (`Goals.check` after each match, from `d.stats`) and `Goals.unlock` keeps a style (`BP_COST` by rarity). GOALS-only skins have `obj: true` and never come from spins. ## Free kick practice

- Mode `freekick` (`MODES.freekick.practice`, FREE KICKS on the start screen): you, a keeper, a wall of 2 to 4 (closer in, bigger) and defenders in the box (`p.wall`, the AI skips them). `startFreeKicks`, `nextFreeKick`, `updateFreeKick`, `fkResult`, `endFreeKicks` in 08_match.js; `m.fk` holds the round. No clock, no Awakening, no rewards; `Game.finishMatch` shows `UI.showPractice` and keeps `Save.data.fkBest`.
- Free kick controls (`p.fkHold`, `freeKickControls`): the camera aims (the target always sits on the goal, `Cam.computeAim`; `Cam.fkFace` turns to it), move X sets `p.fkShot.curve`, pass cycles `FK_TYPES` (CURVE, POWER, DIP), hold shoot for power: off `fkIdeal(D)` by more than `FK_TOL` it flies over the bar or into the wall (`fkLaunch`). DIP uses `ball.gMul` (more gravity). Abilities are off. The keeper is screened until the ball passes the wall and reads knuckleballs late (09_ai.js). `FKHud` (11_fk.js) draws the target, the first part of the path (`fkPath`) and the panel. Test: `node test/freekicks.js`.

## Tutorial

- Mode `tutorial` (`MODES.tutorial`): you, a teammate and one defender, no keeper, no clock. Nov 2026 (CrazyGames quality guidelines, onboarding): only the core in 5 `TUT_STEPS` (move, ball, pass, shoot, done) with short words for keyboard (`k`), controller (`pad`) and phone (`touch`). `TutHud.drawKeys` (11_fk.js) shows pictures of the controls in `#tut-keys`: keycaps that light up while held (`lightKeys`), a mouse with the button to press, controller button names; on phones the button to press glows (`.tut-glow`). Key letters come from `KeyNames` (10_input.js, `navigator.keyboard.getLayoutMap`): Z Q S D on a French AZERTY keyboard. A missed shot comes back to you. `startTutorial`, `tutStep`, `updateTutorial`, `endTutorial` in 08_match.js; `m.tut` holds the state. The others are `p.wall` (the AI skips them).
- First visit: `Game.onboard` (when `Save.data.onboarded` is false, no invite link, not `navigator.webdriver`) lands a brand-new player straight in the tutorial behind `#tut-gate` (CLICK / TAP TO PLAY: the click starts sound, mouse lock and phone fullscreen, `Game.gateGo`). `onboarded` is set at once (also true for anyone who played a match or finished the tutorial), so it happens once.
- SKIP: `#tut-skip` in the box, ENTER, or SKIP TUTORIAL in the pause menu (no confirm) call `Game.skipTutorial`: straight into a quick match. Finishing gives `TUT_REWARD` once (`Save.data.tutorial`) and TUTORIAL DONE with PLAY A QUICK MATCH. NEW? PLAY THE TUTORIAL (`go-tut`) shows until then (or 3 matches), also on How to Play (`howto-tut`). Test: `node test/tutorial.js`.
- First-match tips (`Tips`, 11_fk.js; CrazyGames: a clear UI, teach the core first): while you are new (`Tips.rookie()`: fewer than `ROOKIE_MATCHES` matches and some tip unseen, not online, not the tutorial or free kicks, never under `navigator.webdriver`), body classes `rk-abil`, `rk-moves`, `rk-flow` hide the ability slots and touch buttons, FEINT / SLOW-MO slots and the Flow bar. `TIP_LIST` shows one tip at a time in `#tipbox` (a picture of the key from `KeyPics`, shared with the tutorial; the touch button glows) when it matters: SHOOT (first time with the ball), PASS, SPRINT (walking a while), TACKLE (they have it near you), FEINT (a defender close; reveals `moves`), YOUR POWERS (25s in; reveals `abil`), FLOW (40%; reveals `flow`). 7s between tips, each seen once (`Save.data.tips`). The old 9s line of keys is skipped while tips are on. Test: `node test/tips.js`.
- Start screen (Nov 2026, CrazyGames: clear buttons): a big blue PLAY button first (`go-quick`, a quick match, with a slow shine; it spans both columns on short screens), then RANKED, PLAY WITH FRIENDS, LAWLESS, MORE MODES.
- Letter keys follow the keyboard: `KeyNames.of(code)` (Z Q S D, A for the first power on a French AZERTY keyboard) in the tutorial, tips, HUD slots (`KEYS3` holds codes), Locker, How to Play (`data-kc`, relabelled by `UI.relabelKeys` once the layout is known), the Awakening READY text and the free kick panel.

## Free kick battle

- An offline match still level when golden goal time runs out (`tickClock`, needs `m.human`, not online) goes to `startBattle`: 3 free kicks each (you first), then sudden death; `battleNext` ends it early when one side cannot catch up. It reuses the free kick code (`m.fk.battle`, `fk.shooter`, `fk.bs` goals, `fk.kicks`). Bots take theirs with `botFreeKick`; for theirs you stand in the wall (`p.inWall`: jump only). Battle goals go to `battleGoal`, never the match score. `endBattle` sets `result.res` to win or loss and `result.battle`. Matches with no human (sims, tests) still end as draws. Test: `node test/battle.js`.

## Online server

- `server/server.js` runs online matches; `server/headless.js` loads `src/01` to `src/09` into Node with stand-ins for canvas, sound, camera and particles. Anything those files call must exist there, so keep browser-only code out of them (put it in 10_ and later files) or give it a stand-in in headless.js.
- Hosting: `render.yaml` runs it on Render's free plan (New > Blueprint in Render). The service link is the game and the server in one. Free instances sleep after 15 minutes with no traffic, so `Net.connect` first wakes a remote `wss:` server with a visit to `/health` ("WAKING THE SERVER UP"). The free filesystem is wiped on restarts, so accounts in `DATA_DIR` do not last there. The live server is https://ego-blocks.onrender.com (the game page and the server in one). `npm run build` bakes `wss://ego-blocks.onrender.com/ws` into the page by default (`DEFAULT_SERVER` in build.js; `SCORZ_SERVER=...` changes it, `SCORZ_SERVER=` leaves it out). `Net.serverUrl` uses the page's own server when a SCORZ server sent the page (localhost, home network, or the onrender.com host that `BUILD.server` names: `servedByServer`), else the built-in one, so tests on localhost never touch Render. Another onrender.com name (the free static site scorz.onrender.com: publish directory `docs`, no server) plays on the built-in server.
- `npm run server` starts it on port 8787 (or `PORT`). `MATCH_SECONDS` and `GOLDEN_SECONDS` shorten matches for tests.
- Online modes: quick, koth, volleys, duel (not Lawless yet). The host's BOTS setting (`room.bots`): `fill` (bots in the empty spots), `keepers` (only computer keepers) or `none` (only the friends; `Match` option `bots`, `keeperOf(team)` is null, a field player takes goal kicks). With bots off each team needs a player. Test: `node test/nobots.js`. Room size (server.js): `maxPerTeam` is size - 1 (keepers are never players), so 3v3 holds 4 friends; `join` calls `makeRoom`, which grows the room to `sizeFor(room, 1)` (up to `MAX_SIZE` 5 = 8 friends, `MAX_PLAYERS`) and turns a Duel into Quick Match for a third friend, then sends `info` (a quiet note, `chat-note` buzzes). `settings` never goes below `sizeFor(room)`. Test: `node test/friends.js` (9 clients, one 5v5 match, plus the name filter). Several humans per match: `Match` takes `opts.humans`, each human has `p.ctl`, `p.camYaw`, `p.aimV`, and toasts take the player they are for.
- Drawing online (`Net.applySnapshots`): the picture runs `Net.delay` behind a steady guess of the server's clock (`offMin`, the quickest snapshot lately, on real time). The delay is `NET_DELAY` on a good connection and grows with how late snapshots come (`jit`, up to 0.3s). Past the newest snapshot, players and the ball keep going for up to `NET_COAST` instead of freezing. Test: `node test/netsmooth.js` (fake bumpy connection; `NET_JITTER=0` for a perfect one).
- Tests: `node test/server_proto.js` (protocol) and `node test/online.js` (two real browsers).
- Chat: quick phrases are `QUICK_CHAT` (07_data.js, shared with the server). The server checks every message (`chat()` and `report()` in server.js, the filter in `server/filter.js`: it squeezes double letters, so roots that need theirs, like boob, are `DOUBLE` regexes on the unsqueezed text, or every BOB is blocked; real names that contain a root are `NAMES`, allowed as whole words only): typed chat only for signed-in players, 5 messages per 8 seconds, reports go to `DATA_DIR/reports.jsonl`. Mute is on the player's side (`Net.muted`). Keys: 1 to 4 and T, VIEW + D-pad on a controller, the bubble button on touch. Test: `node test/chat.js`.
- Idle players: `idleStep()` in server.js counts time with no movement or buttons during play (`IDLE_SECONDS`, `IDLE_GRACE`, default 30 + 10), then sets `p.botFor` and takes the player out of `m.humans`. Any active input puts them back. The end message marks `idle`, and `Game.finishMatch` then gives no rewards. Test: `node test/idle.js`.
- FRIENDS panel (Nov 2026, owner: "a place on the side of the screen which you can hide that shows all your friends, and when they are on you can send them the invite, and they can accept mid-match also"): `Friends` in 11_friends.js. `#fr-side` shows on the start screen, the room screen and the pause menu only (`Friends.where`, `Friends.place` runs from `UI.show` and `UI.showPause`), never over a match you are playing. HIDE folds it into a FRIENDS tab on the right edge with the online count (`settings.frOpen`; null = open on screens 1100x640 and up, a tab on phones). Body classes `fr-open`, `fr-lobby` (the panel stops above the rank card) and `fr-online` (the room screen and the pause menu get `padding-right: 330px` on wide screens). Rows: online friends first (INVITE, JOIN when they are in a room), then offline ones (REMOVE, then SURE?), then PLAYERS ONLINE who are not friends yet (ADD FRIEND), and COPY INVITE LINK (makes a room if you have none). A friend coming online gets a note (`ESC TO INVITE` in a match).
- Device friends (no account needed; the free Render server forgets accounts when it restarts): `Save.data.fid` (16 random letters and numbers, made and saved on first load) and `Save.data.friends` ([{ f, n }], up to 60). `hello` carries `fid` and `fr` (your friends' fids). Server: nothing stored; `friendsOf(a, b)` is true when each lists the other (or the old account friends), so one-sided lists show nothing. `{t:'friends'}` answers your online friends (`friendList`: id, f, name, st, room, mine, inv); `Friends.ask` sends it every 10s (and `who` while the panel is open). ## Rules to keep

- Three.js is **r128**, loaded as the global `THREE`. Do not use newer APIs (no `outputColorSpace`, no ES module imports from three).
- No image, model or sound files. Textures and icons are drawn on `<canvas>` with `NearestFilter`. Sounds are synthesized in `Sfx`.
- Music (Nov 2026, CrazyGames: music that fits): `Music` (11_music.js) schedules its notes 0.25s ahead on Sfx's AudioContext through `Sfx.music()` (its own gain straight to the master volume, so mute applies). `Music.set(want)`: `UI.show` wants it on every screen but the HUD, the results screens turn it back on; a match has the crowd. Off with `settings.music` (the lobby SOUND button cycles SOUND ON, MUSIC OFF, SOUND OFF; the pause menu has a Music row), mute (also CrazyGames' via `Portal.applyMute`), a hidden tab, or `navigator.webdriver` (unless `Music.test`). Test: `node test/music.js` (renders the loop offline: peak about 0.5, RMS about 0.04; saves `test/shots/music-preview.wav`).
- Menu sounds come from one place, `UI.bindSounds`: every `button`, `.tab` or `[role=button]` ticks on mouse hover (`Sfx.hover`) and tocks on press (`Sfx.press`: `back` for ids ending `-back`/`-close` or text BACK/CLOSE, `tab` for tabs). A button with `data-sfx="equip"` plays `Sfx.equip` instead. `UI.show` and the pop-ups in `bindSounds` whoosh (`Sfx.open`/`Sfx.close`). `Sfx.click()` is the same tock and skips a repeat in the same moment, so old handlers that still call it do not double up. Keep touch controls as `div`s so they never tock. Test: `node test/uisounds.js`.
- Original names and art only. No names, logos or music from Blue Lock, Minecraft, Roblox or any other brand.
- All three control schemes must keep working: keyboard and mouse, controller, and touch. When you add a player action, wire it into `Input.controls()` (10_input.js), give it a touch button in `10_touch.js`, and list it on the How to Play screen.
- Saved data goes through `Save`. New fields need a default in `Save.defaults()` and sanitizing in `Save.load()`, so old saves keep working.
- Keep `window.EGO` and `Game.stepManual()`. The tests depend on them.
- Layouts must work at 1280x720 (desktop), 844x390 (phone sideways) and 390x844 (phone upright), with no sideways scrolling.
- Player-facing text: short, plain words, no em dashes.
- Keep code off comment lines. Three statements once ended up after a `//` and silently never ran (body height in `Player.animate`, the Flow eyes, two match fields). After script edits, check that no `//` comment runs on into code.

## Testing notes

- Tests run headless Chromium with software WebGL, which is very slow. They advance the game with `EGO.Game.stepManual(frames)` instead of waiting in real time.
- `EGO.simulate(mode, difficulty, seconds, teamSize)` plays a full bot match off screen and returns stats (goals, shots, passes, tackles). Use it to check balance after changing `07_data.js`, `08_match.js` or `09_ai.js`. Healthy: about 3 to 10 goals in a 4 minute match.
- `test/tiers.js` plays its bot matches on the server's copy of the game (`server/headless.js`) before opening the browser: much faster, and 16 games per pair of tiers so it is not luck.
- `node test/stylepower.js [matches] [size] [styles|all] [against]` plays each style's team against ROOKIE (or another style) on the server's copy of the game (`Match` option `teamStyle`). Use 16+ matches; results swing about a goal. Nov 2026 tuning (35 x 55 pitch, short stamina): about 0 to +1.5 goals a match against ROOKIE, rarer a little higher. Free or cheap sprinting is the strongest thing in bot play now (TURBO lost its free sprint; SECOND WIND is strong), then speed. A punched LONG BOMB or IMPACT SHOT only spills loose in the box (`rocket` in the keeper code), so the shooters are not just giving the ball away. To see why a style wins, count its goals by `ball.kick.special` and its ability uses per match.
- Keepers (09_ai.js `keeper`): `crossAt` steps the ball's real path (curve, gravity, bounces) to find where it crosses the line; keepers step across and dive late (`tk < 0.5`, except free kicks) and react later to tricky shots (volley, overhead, knuckle, long bomb; free kick curlers and knuckleballs more). Bots aim their power abilities at the corners (`aimFor(p, m, true)`).
- Nov 2026 feel tuning (`node test/tuning.js`): winding up a shot slows you from half speed to a quarter as the charge fills (`Player.physics`). Bots step into a passing lane only a moment after the pass (`lerp(0.32, 0.12, sk)` seconds) and only when they reach it soon (`decideOff`). Quick Match is 4v4 (`teamSizeFor`) with the line-up `MODES.quick.roster` (GK, DEF, MID, LW); `rolesFor` uses a mode's `roster` when the team size matches, so online rooms of other sizes are unchanged.
- Curve shots (Nov 2026, owner asked "make u can curve while shooting"): hold A/D (the left stick, the touch stick) while you charge. `p.shotCurve` follows `moveX` while shoot is held; `Match.shotLaunch` turns the shot out to one side by up to `CURVE_ANGLE` and sets a steady curve (`curveDecay: 0`) that brings it back onto the aim point, so a curler always ends where you aimed (D bends right, like free kicks). Under `CURVE_MIN` it is a straight shot; full curve is `CURVE_SLOW` slower. `ball.kick.curl` marks it; keepers read a strong one (`> 0.5`) 1.2x later, and good bots curl about a quarter of their shots (`AI.shoot`). HUD: the power bar turns blue with CURVE LEFT/RIGHT and a marker, and `ShotHud` (11_fk.js) draws dots for the first part of the bend (offline only; online the bar uses your local stick). Test: `node test/curve.js`.
- SLOW-MO (Nov 2026, owner: "press C two times ... no one can tackle for that time"): tap C twice within `SLOWMO_TAP` (B twice on a controller, FEINT twice on touch). `updateHuman` checks the double tap before `canAct`, so the second tap works during the feint's step, and a double tap never makes a second feint. `Match.slowMo` needs the ball (never keepers), sets `p.fx.slowmo` for `SLOWMO_TIME` and `p.slowRe` to `SLOWMO_CD`. `Match.slowZones` runs every frame: you stay `tackleProof`, opponents within `SLOWMO_RANGE` (not keepers) get `fx.slowed` (speed x `SLOWMO_SLOW`, and `Player.animate` runs their arms and legs slow too), and it ends as soon as the ball leaves you. The second tap calls `Match.cutFeint`: the first tap's feint stops at once and its charge comes back (playtest: the feint used to keep sliding you about 4.5 blocks sideways through the slow motion, which moved your view and aim, "my cursor just goes to places"), and the zoom is light (`Cam.punch(dur, amt)`, 0.3 here). On a phone, taps just beside a button (`Touch.nearButton`) never count for the double tap that swings the camera to the goal. Bots use it in `decideCarrier` with a defender within 3.2 (about 6 uses in a 4-minute bot match; goals per match did not change). HUD: `#slot-s` (key CC), `#slowfx` (blue screen edge and SLOW-MO; grey SLOWED DOWN while you are caught in someone's), `Sfx.slowmo`, the `hourglass` icon. Online: `me.sr`, `slowmo`/`slowed` in `FX_SENT`, flags 512 (slowed) and 1024 (in slow-mo). Test: `node test/slowmo.js`.
- Nov 2026 bug pass (`node test/bugs.js`): the back of the goal net stops a player who walks into it from behind (it used to throw them to the side of the goal); set pieces only take a fresh press (letting go of a button held when the ball went out used to take the throw-in); a buffered shot is dropped when play stops or you are knocked over (it could fire by itself at the next kickoff); a slide or a boom block that knocks a curler away stops it bending; the third-person camera rises over the bar instead of sitting inside the goal net.
