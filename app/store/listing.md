# Google Play store text for SCORZ

**App name:** SCORZ

**Short description (80 max):**
Blocky 3D football. Pick a style, score wild goals, play with friends.

**Full description:**
SCORZ is fast blocky football where every player has a style.

Start in a small 2v2 match and work your way up to 5v5. Curl shots into the corner, feint past defenders, slow time with SLOW-MO and use your style's three powers. Big plays fill your Flow bar, and sometimes your player Awakens with a signature move.

- Quick matches against bots, with no internet needed
- Ranked: climb from Wood to Obsidian as teams grow from 3v3 to 5v5
- Play online with up to 8 friends using a room code
- 21 styles to unlock, each with its own powers and Awakening
- Free kicks, King of the Hill, Volleys, 1v1 Duels and Lawless mode with items
- Skins, kits, boots, celebrations and emotes, all earned by playing
- Touch controls or a controller
- No ads and nothing to buy
